Ottoline Wigs is free to read. To pay for it, some of the links to retailers may be affiliate links.

## What that means

If you click an affiliate link and then buy something, the retailer may pay us a commission. You pay exactly the same price as you would otherwise.

## What it doesn't change

- Retailers can't pay to be recommended or to change what we say.
- We link to free and NHS options where they exist, even though we earn nothing from them.
- Guides about NHS wigs, VAT relief and charity help carry no affiliate links at all.

If you ever feel a recommendation reads like an advert, [tell us](/pages/contact).
