Hello, I'm Jon, and I live in Surrey.

Ottoline Wigs is a family-run business. We help women find the right wig, whether it's for themselves or for someone in the family.

## Why I started it

For years I helped my grandma buy her wigs. She had her favourites, and when one wore out I'd help her find it again and order a new one.

I never found it easy. The world of wigs was always a grey area to me. Cap types, colour codes, sizes, which shop to trust, what a fair price was. Each time, I had to piece it together again from scratch.

My grandma died during Covid. Afterwards I kept thinking about how hard something so ordinary had been, and how many other families must be doing the same puzzling. That is why this site exists. It's the guide I wish we'd had.

## Who it's for

- Women buying a wig for the first time
- Women who've worn wigs for years and want a straight answer on something
- Sons, daughters, partners and friends helping someone else choose

If you're helping a relative, start with [your first wig](/blogs/guides/your-first-wig-beginners-guide) and the [first wig checklist](/pages/first-wig-checklist).

## How we work

- We read the official sources first: the NHS, HMRC, the cancer and alopecia charities, and the brands' own product and care information.
- Every guide lists its sources at the bottom so you can check them.
- Where we recommend a wig, we say what it costs, where it's sold and who it won't suit.
- Guides on NHS costs and VAT are reviewed whenever the rules change.
- We are not medical professionals. Nothing here replaces advice from your own hospital team or GP.
