Ottoline Wigs is an independent UK guide for women choosing, buying and wearing wigs.

We started it because the first wig is the hardest one to buy. The jargon is baffling, the prices swing wildly, and a lot of the advice online is written by whoever is selling to you. We wanted somewhere calm that explains the choices and tells you what help is out there.

## How we work

- We read the official sources first: the NHS, HMRC, the cancer and alopecia charities, and the brands' own care and product information.
- Every guide lists its sources at the bottom so you can check them.
- Guides on NHS costs and VAT are reviewed whenever the rules change.
- We are not medical professionals, and nothing here replaces advice from your own hospital team or GP.
