## Before you look at wigs

- Ask your hospital team or GP whether you can get an [NHS wig](/blogs/guides/nhs-wigs-who-qualifies-and-how-to-get-one)
- Check whether you qualify for [VAT relief](/blogs/guides/vat-relief-on-wigs)
- Set a budget. See [what wigs cost in the UK](/blogs/guides/how-much-do-wigs-cost-in-the-uk)
- [Measure your head](/blogs/guides/how-to-measure-your-head-for-a-wig) and write the numbers here: ________
- Take a photo of your hair in daylight, front and back
- If you're about to start chemotherapy, keep a small lock of hair for colour matching
{: .checklist }

## Choosing

- Decide on fibre: [synthetic, heat-friendly or human hair](/blogs/guides/synthetic-vs-human-hair-vs-heat-friendly-wigs)
- Decide on [cap type](/blogs/guides/wig-cap-constructions-explained)
- Pick a style close to your own hair for the first one
- Match the colour to your photo, or slightly lighter
- Check the cap size against your measurements
{: .checklist }

## Before you pay

- Read the returns policy. Can you send it back if it's been tried on?
- Ask about a handling or restocking fee
- Ask for the VAT relief form if you qualify
- Note the delivery time if you need it by a certain date
{: .checklist }

## What to buy with it

- A wig stand
- Wig shampoo (and conditioner for longer styles)
- A wide-tooth comb
- A wig cap or grip, if your scalp is bare or the wig slips. See [caps, grips and tapes](/blogs/guides/wig-caps-grips-and-tapes)
{: .checklist }

## When it arrives

- Try it on over a wig cap, with the labels still on
- Check the fit: snug, not tight, and it stays put when you nod
- Look at the colour by a window, not under a bulb
- Decide within the returns period
- Book a trim with a hairdresser who cuts wigs, if it needs shaping
{: .checklist }
