If your hair loss is caused by a medical condition or treatment, you may be able to buy a wig without paying VAT. Type in the price you see and we'll show what you'd pay.

Whether you qualify depends on HMRC's rules, and you have to claim when you buy. Read [VAT relief on wigs](/blogs/guides/vat-relief-on-wigs) first if you haven't.
