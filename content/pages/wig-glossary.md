Wig shopping comes with its own vocabulary. Here's what the words mean, with a link to the full guide where there is one.

## A to C

**Adjusters**
: Small Velcro tabs or hooks at the nape that tighten or loosen the cap by a centimetre or two. See [how to measure your head](/blogs/guides/how-to-measure-your-head-for-a-wig).

**Average**
: The standard wig size, and the one most styles are made in. The other sizes are petite and large.

**Base**
: The part of a topper or hairpiece that sits on your head. Its size decides how much it covers. See [hair toppers explained](/blogs/guides/hair-toppers-explained).

**Blend**
: Two or more shades woven evenly through a wig, to give the depth of natural hair. Also called a mix.

**Cap**
: The inside of the wig, which the hair is attached to. It decides how the wig feels and how natural it looks. See [cap constructions explained](/blogs/guides/wig-cap-constructions-explained).

**Colour ring**
: A ring of hair samples in every shade a brand makes, used to choose a colour in real light. Some retailers lend them.

**Cutting in**
: Having a hairdresser trim and thin a wig to suit your face. See [how to make a wig look natural](/blogs/guides/how-to-make-a-wig-look-natural).

## D to H

**Density**
: How much hair a wig has. Too much is one of the things that makes a wig look like a wig.

**Double monofilament**
: A monofilament top with a second soft layer underneath, so the knots don't touch your scalp.

**Ear tabs**
: Small flat tabs in front of each ear that help you centre the wig and keep it level.

**Grip band**
: A soft band worn under the wig to stop it sliding. See [wig caps, grips and tapes](/blogs/guides/wig-caps-grips-and-tapes).

**Hand-tied**
: A cap where every hair is knotted in by hand onto soft mesh. The softest and most natural-moving kind, and the dearest.

**HC2 and HC3**
: NHS Low Income Scheme certificates. HC2 gives full help with health costs, including NHS wigs. HC3 gives partial help. See [NHS wigs](/blogs/guides/nhs-wigs-who-qualifies-and-how-to-get-one).

**Heat-friendly**
: Synthetic fibre that can be styled with tools on a low setting. Also called heat-resistant or heat-defiant. See [synthetic vs human hair vs heat-friendly](/blogs/guides/synthetic-vs-human-hair-vs-heat-friendly-wigs).

**Human hair**
: Wigs made from real hair. They can be styled like your own, cost more and need styling after each wash.

## L to P

**Lace front**
: A strip of fine lace along the front hairline with hairs tied in one by one, giving a soft, natural hairline.

**Liner**
: A thin cotton or bamboo cap worn under a wig for comfort. See [wigs for sensitive scalps](/blogs/guides/wigs-for-sensitive-scalps).

**Modacrylic**
: The synthetic fibre most wigs are made from. It's the word the NHS uses for its standard synthetic wigs.

**Monofilament**
: Fine see-through mesh with hairs tied in by hand, so the scalp shows through and the parting looks real. Often shortened to mono. It can cover the parting, the crown or the whole top.

**Nape**
: The back of your neck at the hairline. It's where a wig's adjusters sit and where tangles start.

**Open cap**
: Another name for a wefted cap, because of the gaps between the rows of hair.

**Petite**
: The smallest standard wig size.

## R to W

**Rooted**
: A shade that's darker at the root than along the length, like coloured hair with a little regrowth. Also called shaded. See [rooted, highlighted and blended shades](/blogs/guides/rooted-highlighted-and-blended-shades-explained).

**Swatch**
: A small sample of hair in one shade, for checking a colour before you buy.

**Synthetic**
: Wigs made from man-made fibre. The style is set in, so they need no styling after washing, but ordinary synthetic can't take heat.

**Tape**
: Double-sided tape that holds a wig to the skin. Look for hypoallergenic tape made for wigs.

**Tipped**
: A shade with lighter ends.

**Topper**
: A small hairpiece that clips onto the top of the head to cover thinning. Also called a top piece or hair enhancer. See [wig or topper?](/blogs/guides/thinning-hair-wig-or-topper)

**VAT relief**
: An exemption that lets people who are chronically sick or disabled buy certain goods, including wigs, without VAT. See [VAT relief on wigs](/blogs/guides/vat-relief-on-wigs).

**Weft**
: A strip of material with hair sewn onto it. Wefts are stitched in rows to make a basic cap.

**Wefted cap**
: A cap made of rows of wefts with gaps between. Light, cool and the least expensive.

**Wig cap**
: A thin stretchy cap worn under a wig to hold your own hair flat. Not the same as the cap of the wig itself.

**Wig stand**
: A stand that holds a wig's shape while it dries and when you're not wearing it. See [how to store and travel with a wig](/blogs/guides/how-to-store-and-travel-with-a-wig).
