This page explains what this site does with your information.

## What we collect

At the moment the site collects no personal information. There are no accounts, no comment forms and no sign-up forms.

## Cookies

The site itself sets no cookies. If you follow a link to a retailer, that retailer's site may set cookies of its own, including ones that record that you arrived from us.

## Emails you send us

If you email us, we keep your message only for as long as we need to reply and deal with your question.

## Changes

If we add analytics, a newsletter or anything else that uses your information, we will update this page first.
