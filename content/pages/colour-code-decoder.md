Wig colours come with codes like **RL12/22SS** or **12FS8**. They look like nonsense until you know the pattern. Type one in and we'll break it down.

It also reads shade names, such as *Stone Grey Rooted*.

For the full explanation, see [how to choose a wig colour and read colour codes](/blogs/guides/how-to-choose-a-wig-colour).
