You don't have to work all this out alone. These organisations help with wigs and hair loss in the UK. We've said what each one actually does, so you can go straight to the right one.

We have no connection with any of them and earn nothing from listing them.

## Free and low-cost wigs

NHS wigs
:   Free in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, and for some people in England. Others in England pay a set charge. Your hospital team refers you. See our guide to [NHS wigs](/blogs/guides/nhs-wigs-who-qualifies-and-how-to-get-one), or the [NHS page](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/help-with-health-costs/wigs-and-fabric-supports-on-the-nhs/).

NHS Low Income Scheme
:   If money is tight and you live in England, you may get help with the NHS wig charge by filling in form HC1. [NHS Business Services Authority](https://www.nhsbsa.nhs.uk/nhs-low-income-scheme).

Little Princess Trust
:   Free human hair wigs for children and young people up to the age of 24. [littleprincesses.org.uk](https://www.littleprincesses.org.uk/)

Wig Bank
:   Takes in wigs people no longer need. Wig banks sell or hire them at a lower price. Also the place to send a wig you've finished with. [wigbank.com](https://www.wigbank.com/)

VAT relief
:   Not a charity, but worth a sixth off the price if your hair loss has a medical cause. See [VAT relief on wigs](/blogs/guides/vat-relief-on-wigs).

## Advice and support

Cancer Hair Care
:   Free advice on hair loss, scalp cooling and hair care before, during and after cancer treatment. Includes specialist advice for Afro-textured hair. Also recycles wigs and headscarves. Helpline 0300 102 2525. [cancerhaircare.co.uk](https://www.cancerhaircare.co.uk/)

Look Good Feel Better
:   Free workshops in hospitals and support centres, and video tutorials on hair loss, skin and make-up, for people having cancer treatment. Phone 01372 747500.

Macmillan Cancer Support
:   Clear information on wigs during cancer treatment and on help with health costs. [Wigs for cancer treatment](https://www.macmillan.org.uk/cancer-information-and-support/impacts-of-cancer/hair-loss/wigs-for-cancer-treatment)

Breast Cancer Now
:   A free nurse helpline on 0808 800 6000, and a list of headwear and wig suppliers. [Wigs, scarves and headwear](https://breastcancernow.org/about-breast-cancer/treatment/chemotherapy/chemotherapy-side-effects/wigs-scarves-and-headwear)

Cancer Black Care
:   Support and advice for Black people affected by cancer, including help with choosing a wig. [cancerblackcare.org.uk](https://www.cancerblackcare.org.uk/)

Alopecia UK
:   Support groups (local and online), a downloadable guide to wigs, and a charter you can show your hospital if you're struggling to get an NHS wig for alopecia. [alopecia.org.uk](https://www.alopecia.org.uk/)

British Hair and Nail Society
:   Information on concealers that disguise thinning hair, if you're not ready for a wig. [bhns.org.uk](https://bhns.org.uk/)

## Finding a wig fitter or shop near you

We don't list individual shops. Shops open, close and change hands, and a wrong entry could send you on a wasted journey. These three routes will find someone current.

1. **Ask your hospital team.** If you're having treatment, your clinical nurse specialist can refer you to the NHS wig fitting service. Fitters can sometimes visit you on the ward.
2. **Use the My New Hair salon finder.** Enter your postcode and it lists salons across the UK whose hairdressers are trained to supply, cut and style wigs. [mynewhair.org salon finder](http://www.mynewhair.org/salon-finder/)
3. **Ask a charity for its supplier list.** Breast Cancer Now keeps one. Alopecia UK support groups are a good place to ask which local shops people rate.

When you ring a shop, ask three things: do I need an appointment, is there a private fitting room, and is there a fitting fee if I don't buy?

See also [buying a wig online vs in a shop](/blogs/guides/buying-a-wig-online-vs-in-a-shop).
