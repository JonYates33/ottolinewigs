## Money

**Can I get a wig on the NHS?**
Yes. They are free in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. In England some people get one free and others pay a set charge. See [NHS wigs](/blogs/guides/nhs-wigs-who-qualifies-and-how-to-get-one).

**What does an NHS wig cost in England?**
From April 2026: £80.15 for a stock synthetic wig, £212.35 for a part human hair wig and £310.55 for a full human hair wig made to order.

**Do I have to pay VAT?**
Not if your hair loss comes from a medical condition or its treatment. You claim when you buy, not afterwards. See [VAT relief on wigs](/blogs/guides/vat-relief-on-wigs).

**How much is a decent wig?**
A good synthetic wig is usually £100 to £300. See [what wigs cost in the UK](/blogs/guides/how-much-do-wigs-cost-in-the-uk).

## Choosing

**Synthetic or human hair?**
Synthetic for most people, most of the time. It's cheaper and keeps its style. See [synthetic vs human hair](/blogs/guides/synthetic-vs-human-hair-vs-heat-friendly-wigs).

**How do I know my size?**
Measure round your head with a tape. See [how to measure](/blogs/guides/how-to-measure-your-head-for-a-wig).

**Will it look like a wig?**
Fit and colour decide that more than price does. See [how to make a wig look natural](/blogs/guides/how-to-make-a-wig-look-natural).

**Do I need a whole wig, or would a topper do?**
If your hair is thin on top but fine at the sides and back, a topper may be enough. See [wig or topper?](/blogs/guides/thinning-hair-wig-or-topper)

**What do the colour codes mean?**
Try the [colour code decoder](/pages/colour-code-decoder).

## Wearing

**Will it fall off?**
A wig that fits stays on. Grips and tapes add security if you want it. See [how to keep a wig secure](/blogs/guides/how-to-put-on-a-wig-and-keep-it-secure).

**Is it hot?**
It can be. Short styles and open caps are cooler. See [wearing a wig in hot weather](/blogs/guides/wearing-a-wig-in-hot-weather-gym-and-holiday).

**Can I sleep or swim in it?**
Best not. Both shorten its life.

## Looking after it

**How often should I wash it?**
About every six to eight wears. See the [wig care routine](/pages/wig-care-routine).

**How long will it last?**
A synthetic wig worn daily lasts some months. See [how long do wigs last?](/blogs/guides/how-long-do-wigs-last)
