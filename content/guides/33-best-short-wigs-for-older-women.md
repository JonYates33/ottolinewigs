Short wigs are the lightest, the coolest and the least work. They also last longest, because nothing rubs on your collar.

Our pick is the **Winner by Raquel Welch** at about 50g. If you want a parting that looks like scalp and still want it light, the **Tango by Raquel Welch** weighs 58g with a monofilament top.

## How to choose

- **Wefted or monofilament?** Wefted tops are airy and cheaper. Monofilament tops show a natural parting and feel softer. See [cap constructions explained](/blogs/guides/wig-cap-constructions-explained).
- **Lace front or fringe?** A lace front lets you brush the hair back. A fringe hides the edge and costs less.
- **Weight.** Under 70g is light.
- **Size.** Short styles show a poor fit quickly. [Measure first](/blogs/guides/how-to-measure-your-head-for-a-wig).

## Questions people ask

**Are short wigs cooler to wear?**

Yes. There's less fibre and usually a lighter cap. See [wearing a wig in hot weather](/blogs/guides/wearing-a-wig-in-hot-weather-gym-and-holiday).

**Do short wigs suit a round face?**

Height at the crown and some texture help. See [styles for your face shape](/blogs/guides/best-wig-styles-for-your-face-shape).

**Can a hairdresser trim a wig?**

Yes, and it's worth it. Ask one who has cut wigs before.

**How do I wash one?**

Cool water, wig shampoo, and dry it on a stand. See [how to wash a synthetic wig](/blogs/guides/how-to-wash-a-synthetic-wig).
