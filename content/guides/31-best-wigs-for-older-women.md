## How to choose

Four things matter more as you get older.

- **Weight.** You'll wear it all day. Short wigs are lightest. Anything under 70g is light. See [choosing a length](/blogs/guides/choosing-a-wig-length).
- **A soft cap.** Monofilament and hand-tied caps are kinder to thin skin than basic wefts. See [cap constructions explained](/blogs/guides/wig-cap-constructions-explained).
- **The right size.** With less hair underneath, many women need a petite. [Measure first](/blogs/guides/how-to-measure-your-head-for-a-wig).
- **Easy fastenings.** Velcro tabs are easier on stiff fingers than hooks.

## Questions people ask

**What is the most comfortable wig for an older woman?**

A short, light wig with a soft cap. Of the six here, the Balance Petite has the softest cap and the Winner is the lightest.

**Should I choose grey?**

If your own hair is grey, a grey blend usually looks most natural against your skin. See [grey and silver wigs](/blogs/guides/grey-and-silver-wigs).

**Do I need a petite size?**

Measure round your head. Under about 54cm, a petite is worth trying. Many women find they need one once their own hair has thinned.

**How long will a synthetic wig last?**

Worn every day, around four to six months. Two wigs worn in turn last longer than one. See [how long do wigs last?](/blogs/guides/how-long-do-wigs-last)

**Can I buy one for my mother?**

Yes. Measure her head, take a photo of her hair in daylight, and check the returns policy before you order.
