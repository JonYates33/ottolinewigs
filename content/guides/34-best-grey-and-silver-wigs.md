## How to choose

- **Match the back of your head.** Hair is usually darker at the nape and lighter at the front. Match the lighter part.
- **Look for a blend.** Names with "mix", "mist" or "rooted" usually mean several tones. See [rooted and blended shades](/blogs/guides/rooted-highlighted-and-blended-shades-explained).
- **Check it in daylight.** Screen colours mislead. Some retailers lend colour rings.
- **Warm or cool?** Creamy whites flatter warm skin. Silvers and pewters flatter cool skin. See [how to choose a wig colour](/blogs/guides/how-to-choose-a-wig-colour).

## Who should look elsewhere

- **You're still mostly brown or blonde.** A shade with grey highlights may suit better than full grey.
- **You want grey human hair.** It is rare and expensive. Most grey wigs are synthetic for that reason.

## Questions people ask

**Will a grey wig make me look older?**

A flat, dull grey can. A bright blend usually looks fresher than a dark dye that no longer suits your skin.

**Do grey synthetic wigs go yellow?**

They can pick up colour from smoke, cooking and hard water. Wash with wig shampoo and store away from sunlight. See [how to store a wig](/blogs/guides/how-to-store-and-travel-with-a-wig).

**What do the colour codes mean?**

Numbers in the 50s and 60s are usually greys and whites. See [how to read colour codes](/blogs/guides/how-to-choose-a-wig-colour).

**Can I blend a topper into grey hair?**

Yes, but the match must be close. See [hair toppers explained](/blogs/guides/hair-toppers-explained).
