Where your wig spends the sixteen hours a day it isn't on your head matters nearly as much as how you wash it. A wig left in a heap on the dressing table loses its shape. One kept properly looks fresh for months longer.

## Overnight

**Use a stand.** Elegant Wigs' care instructions say wigs should be stored on a stand to keep their shape. A simple open or folding stand is best, because it lets air reach the inside of the cap.

**Let it air first.** If your head has been warm, give the cap a few minutes to dry before you put the wig away.

**Keep it away from:**

- direct sunlight, which fades colour
- radiators and other heat
- steam from the bathroom or kitchen
- pets and small children, who find wigs fascinating

## While it's drying

Dry a washed wig on a stand too. Envy Wigs adds a caution: a wig can lose its shape if it's pulled down over a stand or head form while still wet. Rest it on lightly and let it hang.

## Between seasons, or a wig you've stopped wearing

1. **Wash it and let it dry completely.** Never store a damp wig. It will smell and can go mouldy.
2. **Detangle it gently.**
3. **Put it in a hairnet**, if it came with one, to hold the style.
4. **Store it in its box or a breathable bag**, somewhere cool, dry and dark.
5. **Don't stack anything on top.**

If you have several, label the boxes with the style and colour. You'll be glad of it later.

## Packing for a trip

**Hand luggage if you can.** Suitcases go missing. A wig you depend on shouldn't be in one.

**Best:** a wig travel box or the box it came in, with the wig in its hairnet.

**Fine for a short trip:** turn the wig gently inside out, tuck the ends inside, slip it into a bag and lay it on top of your clothes where nothing will crush it.

**Try before you go.** Simply Wigs suggests testing the wig and any accessories you plan to travel with beforehand, particularly if you're going somewhere hot.

### What to take

- A folding stand
- A wide-tooth comb
- Travel-size wig shampoo and conditioner. Liquids in hand luggage have to meet the airport's rules.
- A spare liner or grip band
- Tape, if you use it
- A second wig, if you have one. Joseph's Wigs suggests three for a holiday, including an older one for the pool.

## When you arrive

Take the wig out, give it a shake and put it on its stand. Most synthetic styles spring back within an hour or so.

Simply Wigs' travel guide gives three places never to leave a wig: in a hot car, on a sunny windowsill, or packed away while still damp.

## At the airport

A wig goes through security like anything else you're wearing, and you wouldn't normally be asked to remove it. If staff ever need a closer look and you'd prefer privacy, ask for a private room.

Flying is dry, and wigs pick up static on planes. A light mist of conditioning spray when you land sorts it out.

## Quick answers

**Can I keep my wig on a polystyrene head?** Yes, though an open stand airs the cap better. A head is useful if you style the wig on it.

**Can I hang it on a hook?** Not for long. It stretches the cap.

**Can I keep it in the bathroom?** Best not. Steam and damp aren't good for it.
