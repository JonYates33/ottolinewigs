## How to choose

- **Start with synthetic.** It keeps its style after washing. See [synthetic vs human hair](/blogs/guides/synthetic-vs-human-hair-vs-heat-friendly-wigs).
- **Stay close to your own hair.** Same sort of length and colour. You'll feel like yourself sooner.
- **Measure your head.** Most returns are about size. [How to measure](/blogs/guides/how-to-measure-your-head-for-a-wig).
- **Don't overspend.** £100 to £200 buys a good first wig. See [what wigs cost in the UK](/blogs/guides/how-much-do-wigs-cost-in-the-uk).
- **Check the returns policy before you order.** Some brands carry a handling fee.

## Questions people ask

**Is it all right to buy a first wig online?**

Yes, if you measure your head, match your colour in daylight and check you can send it back. A shop visit is still the surest way to learn your size.

**What else do I need?**

A wig stand, wig shampoo and a wide-tooth comb. A wig grip helps if it slips. See [caps, grips and tapes](/blogs/guides/wig-caps-grips-and-tapes).

**Will people be able to tell?**

Less than you fear. Fit and colour matter most. See [how to make a wig look natural](/blogs/guides/how-to-make-a-wig-look-natural).

**How do I put it on?**

Front first, then pull down at the back, then line up the ear tabs. See [how to put on a wig](/blogs/guides/how-to-put-on-a-wig-and-keep-it-secure).

**Should my first wig be human hair?**

Usually not. It costs far more and needs styling after every wash.
