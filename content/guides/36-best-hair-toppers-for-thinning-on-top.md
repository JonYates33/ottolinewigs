## How to choose

- **Measure the thin area.** The base should be a little bigger, because the clips need healthy hair to grip.
- **Match your own hair length.** A topper blends into your hair, so it must be about the same length or be trimmed to it.
- **Match the colour closely.** This matters more than with a wig. See [how to choose a wig colour](/blogs/guides/how-to-choose-a-wig-colour).
- **Monofilament or lace on top looks most natural at the parting.**
- **Clips need hair to hold.** If the hair around the thin patch is very fine or fragile, a wig may be kinder.

More detail in [hair toppers explained](/blogs/guides/hair-toppers-explained).

## Who should look elsewhere

- **Thinning all over, or at the hairline.** A wig will look more natural. See [best wigs for older women](/blogs/guides/best-wigs-for-older-women).
- **Patchy loss or a tender scalp.** Clips can pull. Ask your GP or dermatologist first.
- **You want to try before buying.** A wig shop or trained hairdresser can match colour in person. See the [help and support directory](/pages/help-and-support-directory).

## Questions people ask

**Will the clips damage my hair?**

They can pull if you clip in the same place every day. Move them slightly and take the topper off at night.

**Can a hairdresser cut a topper to blend in?**

Yes, and it makes a big difference. Use one who has cut hairpieces before.

**Can I get a topper on the NHS?**

NHS provision is for wigs. Ask your hospital team what your trust offers.

**Do I pay VAT?**

VAT relief on wigs depends on your condition. See [VAT relief on wigs](/blogs/guides/vat-relief-on-wigs) and ask the retailer whether they apply it to toppers.
