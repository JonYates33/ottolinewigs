Shorter than you'd hope, is the honest answer, and it's better to know that before you buy. A wig worn every day is a working thing, like a pair of shoes. Plan for that and you won't feel cheated when it wears out.

## What to expect

| Type | Typical life with daily wear | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Synthetic | 4 to 8 months | Macmillan |
| Synthetic | 6 to 9 months | Cancer Research UK |
| Human hair | 1 to 3 years | Cancer Research UK |

Heat-friendly synthetic usually sits at the lower end, because the fibre tangles and roughens sooner.

These figures are for wearing one wig daily. Wear it three days a week and it'll last roughly twice as long.

## What wears a wig out

**Friction.** The main one. Wigs.com explains that synthetic fibre frizzes from friction and tension, where it rubs against your clothes and body or gets pulled during brushing. That's why the nape and the ends go first.

**Heat.** Hairdryers, oven doors, hot water, even a sunny windowsill.

**Washing wrongly.** Envy Wigs makes a point worth knowing: washing too often stresses the fibre, but washing too rarely lets dirt and oil build up and break it down. Both shorten its life.

**Length.** Longer hair has more to rub against.

**Sleeping in it.** Hours of pillow friction every night.

## Eight habits that add months

1. **Own two and take turns.** Each one rests, and you're never without on wash day. Two wigs worn alternately tend to outlast two worn one after the other.
2. **Comb the nape after every wear.** Headcovers' care guide says a gentle pass each time stops matting from building into something harder to fix.
3. **Wash it properly.** Cool water, wig shampoo, no rubbing. See [how to wash a synthetic wig](/blogs/guides/how-to-wash-a-synthetic-wig).
4. **Keep it on a stand** when you're not wearing it.
5. **Keep it away from heat.** Step back when you open the oven.
6. **Use a conditioning spray** on the ends of longer styles.
7. **Wear it up, or over one shoulder,** with coats and scarves.
8. **Take it off at home.** Every hour it isn't on your head is an hour it isn't wearing out. Your scalp will thank you too.

## Signs it's time for a new one

- The ends stay frizzy and dry even after washing.
- The nape is matted and won't comb out.
- It's lost its shape or looks thin.
- The cap has stretched and the wig no longer feels secure.
- The colour has gone dull.

A wig past its best is still useful. Keep it for gardening, the gym, the pool, or under a hat.

## Thinking about cost properly

Price per month tells you more than the price on the ticket.

| Wig | Price | Life | Cost a month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Synthetic | £180 | 6 months | £30 |
| Human hair | £900 | 2 years | £37.50 |

Those are illustrations, not quotes. But they show why a dearer wig isn't always the more expensive choice, and why looking after a wig is worth real money. An extra two months from a £180 wig is £60 saved a year.
